# First Steps with Donezy

Welcome to Donezy! This guide will help you get started with the platform in minutes.

## Creating Your Account

1. Go to [donezy.io](https://app.donezy.io)
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email address
5. Set up your profile with your name and photo
6. Create or join an organization

## Your First Login

After signing up, you'll land on the **Home Dashboard**. This is your command center where you can:
- See tasks assigned to you
- Start tracking time
- View recent activity
- Access quick shortcuts

## Setting Up Your Profile

Go to **Settings** → **Account Settings** to:
- Update your name and email
- Upload a profile picture
- Change your password
- Set your timezone
- Enable two-factor authentication

## Creating Your First Task

1. Navigate to **Tasks** or use the **+** button in the sidebar
2. Click **New Task**
3. Enter a task title
4. Choose a project (or create one)
5. Set a due date and priority
6. Assign it to yourself or team members
7. Click **Create Task**

## Creating Your First Project

1. Go to **Projects**
2. Click **New Project**
3. Enter project name and description
4. Set start and end dates
5. Add team members
6. Click **Create Project**

Your project will appear in your dashboard and you can start adding tasks to it.

## Inviting Team Members

1. Go to **Settings** → **Organization Settings**
2. Click **Invite Users**
3. Enter their email addresses
4. Select their role (Admin, Manager, Member, or Viewer)
5. Click **Send Invites**

They'll receive an email and can join your organization.

## Starting Time Tracking

Time tracking helps you understand where your time goes:

1. Go to **Time Tracking** or your **Dashboard**
2. Find the **Active Timer** card
3. Select a project and task
4. Click **Start Timer**
5. When done, click **Stop** and save

You can also manually add time entries for work completed earlier.

## Customizing Your Dashboard

Create a personalized dashboard to see what matters to you:

1. Go to **Dashboards**
2. Click **New Dashboard**
3. Choose from available cards (Tasks, Time Tracking, Notes, etc.)
4. Arrange them to your preference
5. Save your dashboard

## Setting Notification Preferences

Control how you're notified:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Notification Preferences**
2. Choose which events trigger notifications
3. Select in-app, email, or both
4. Save your preferences

## Quick Tips

- Use **keyboard shortcuts** for faster navigation (see Keyboard Shortcuts guide)
- Pin your most-used projects for quick access
- Use **filters** on task lists to focus on what matters
- Create **custom fields** to track information specific to your needs
- Enable **Google Chat integration** to get Donezy notifications in your messaging app
