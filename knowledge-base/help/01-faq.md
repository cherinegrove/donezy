# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common questions about Donezy.

## Getting Started

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Go to [donezy.io](https://app.donezy.io), click "Sign Up", enter your email and password, verify your email, and you're ready to go. See [First Steps](../getting-started/01-first-steps.md).

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes! Go to **Settings** → **Account Settings** → **Email**. You'll need to verify the new email.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the link in the reset email.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to **Settings** → **Account Settings** → **Delete Account**. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Teams & Organizations

**Q: How do I invite team members?**
A: Go to **Settings** → **Team Management** → **Invite Users**, enter their email, select role, and send. They'll receive an invitation email.

**Q: What's the difference between Manager and Member roles?**
A: Managers can create projects and assign tasks to team members. Members can only edit their own tasks. See [Roles & Permissions](../getting-started/02-roles-and-permissions.md).

**Q: Can I have multiple organizations?**
A: Yes! You can be part of multiple organizations. Switch using the organization switcher in the sidebar.

**Q: How do I remove a team member?**
A: Go to **Settings** → **Team Management**, find the person, and click **Remove**. They lose access immediately.

**Q: Can I change someone's role?**
A: Yes! Go to **Settings** → **Team Management**, click their role dropdown, select new role.

## Projects & Tasks

**Q: Can I create a project from a template?**
A: Yes! Go to **Projects** → **Templates**, find a template, and click **Use Template**.

**Q: What's the difference between assigning and collaborating?**
A: Assigned person is responsible for the task. Collaborators are notified and can help but aren't the primary owner.

**Q: How do I delete a task?**
A: Open the task, click the menu (⋯), and click **Delete**. This is permanent.

**Q: Can I archive instead of delete?**
A: Yes! Archive keeps the task hidden but recoverable. Better than deleting if you might need it later.

**Q: How do I create recurring tasks?**
A: When creating a task, enable **Recurring** and choose frequency (daily, weekly, monthly).

**Q: Can I link tasks to show dependencies?**
A: Yes! Open a task, click **Links**, choose link type (Blocks, Depends on, Related), and select the related task.

## Time Tracking

**Q: Why is the timer not tracking?**
A: Check your internet connection, verify the browser tab hasn't closed, or reload the page.

**Q: Can I track time on more than one task simultaneously?**
A: No, only one timer can run at a time. Starting a new timer stops the previous one.

**Q: How do I add time retroactively?**
A: Go to **Time Tracking**, click **Add Manual Entry**, select project/task, set date and hours, and save.

**Q: Can I edit a time entry after saving?**
A: Yes! Go to **Time Tracking** → **Time Log**, find the entry, click to open, edit, and save.

**Q: How do I mark time as billable?**
A: When saving time, toggle the **Billable** switch. Billable time appears on invoices.

**Q: What's the difference between billable and non-billable time?**
A: Billable time is charged to clients at their hourly rate. Non-billable is internal work not invoiced.

**Q: Does the timer work if I close my browser?**
A: On web: No, timer stops when browser closes. On mobile: Continues if app is in background.

## Communication

**Q: How do I mention someone in a comment?**
A: Type `@` followed by their name. They'll be notified of the mention.

**Q: Can I edit a message after posting?**
A: Yes! Click the message, click the menu (⋯), and click **Edit**.

**Q: How do I create a channel?**
A: Go to **Channels** → **New Channel**, name it, add members, and create.

**Q: Can I delete a channel?**
A: Only the channel creator (or admin) can delete channels. Click the channel menu and select **Delete**.

**Q: How do I mute notifications from a channel?**
A: Click the channel name, click the bell icon, and toggle mute.

## Dashboards & Reports

**Q: Can I create a custom dashboard?**
A: Yes! Go to **Dashboards** → **New Dashboard**, add cards, arrange them, and save.

**Q: How do I share a dashboard?**
A: Open the dashboard, click **Share**, and send the link to teammates.

**Q: Can I export reports?**
A: Yes! In most reports, click **Export** to download as CSV or PDF.

**Q: How often do reports update?**
A: Reports update in real-time, but some summaries may have a slight delay (usually seconds).

## Integrations

**Q: How do I connect Google Chat?**
A: Go to **Integrations** → **Google Chat**, click "Connect", authorize, and choose which notifications to send.

**Q: Can I turn off Slack notifications?**
A: Yes! Go to **Integrations** → **Slack**, toggle off the notifications you don't want.

**Q: How do I create a task from Slack?**
A: Use `/donezy create` in Slack to create a task that appears in Donezy.

**Q: Do integrations sync automatically?**
A: Yes! Most integrations sync in real-time or within seconds.

## Mobile & Extensions

**Q: Does the mobile app work offline?**
A: Partially. You can view cached data but can't create or edit tasks. Changes sync when online.

**Q: How do I install the Chrome extension?**
A: Search "Donezy" in Chrome Web Store, click "Add to Chrome", and sign in.

**Q: Can I track time with the extension?**
A: Yes! Click the extension icon, select project/task, and click "Start Timer".

**Q: Will my phone number be used?**
A: No, Donezy doesn't need or use phone numbers.

## Billing & Subscriptions

**Q: How do I update my payment method?**
A: Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Payment Method**, and update your card details.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription?**
A: Yes! Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Subscription**, and click **Cancel Subscription**.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**
A: Refund policy varies. Contact [support@donezy.io](mailto:support@donezy.io) for details.

**Q: Can I upgrade/downgrade anytime?**
A: Yes! Changes take effect immediately and are prorated.

## Permissions & Security

**Q: Can I make a task private?**
A: Not exactly, but you can limit visibility by only assigning to specific people.

**Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication?**
A: Go to **Settings** → **Account Settings** → **Security**, and toggle **Two-Factor Authentication**.

**Q: Is my data encrypted?**
A: Yes! Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS. Data at rest is encrypted in our database.

**Q: Can I export all my data?**
A: Yes! Go to **Settings** → **Data** → **Download my data** to get a complete export.

## Notifications

**Q: How do I change notification preferences?**
A: Go to **Settings** → **Notification Preferences**, choose what you want to be notified about.

**Q: Can I disable notifications for specific projects?**
A: Go to project settings → **Notifications**, toggle off what you don't want.

**Q: Why am I getting notifications I disabled?**
A: Check both organization and project-level notification settings.

**Q: Can I schedule quiet hours?**
A: Yes! Go to **Notification Preferences** → **Quiet Hours** and set your time range.

## Technical Issues

**Q: The app is running slow. What do I do?**
A: Try these:
1. Clear your browser cache
2. Disable browser extensions
3. Close other tabs
4. Refresh the page
5. Try a different browser

**Q: A feature isn't working. What should I do?**
A: 1. Refresh the page
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try incognito mode
4. Contact [support@donezy.io](mailto:support@donezy.io)

**Q: Why can't I see recent changes?**
A: The page may be cached. Try refreshing (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R).

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions).

## Account Issues

**Q: I'm locked out of my account. What do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page to reset your password.

**Q: Someone else is using my account. What should I do?**
A: Change your password immediately, enable two-factor authentication, and contact support.

**Q: How do I transfer ownership of a project?**
A: Open the project, go to **Settings** → **Team**, find the person, and click to change their role to **Owner**.

**Q: Can I download my data?**
A: Yes! Go to **Settings** → **Account** → **Download My Data**.
